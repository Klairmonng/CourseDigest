### Key Market Trends (Summer 2026)

1. **Futuristic Sport & Wraparound Silhouettes**  
   Driven by the ongoing convergence of luxury fashion and high-performance athletic wear, high-coverage wraparound frames with single curved lenses have emerged as the season's dominant statement piece, offering a sleek, modern aesthetic.
2. **Architectural Acetate & Bold Statements**  
   Chunky, heavy-gauge acetate frames remain a high-impact runway staple. These confident, structural designs appeal to consumers seeking high-visibility accessories that anchor contemporary wardrobes.
3. **Refined Retro & Elevated Cat-Eye Elegance**  
   A perennial driver of premium margins, sophisticated vintage silhouettes—particularly 1950s-inspired cat-eye curves—continue to thrive, capturing the lucrative market segment seeking effortless, everyday glamour.
